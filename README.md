# PUI89-Research

**Independent research programme | English · ภาษาไทย**

PUI89-Research develops evidence-based, resource-aware and reliable AI research across six product-oriented projects. The goal is to turn research questions into reproducible methods, measurable experiments and transparently reported evidence.

**ภาษาไทย:** [อ่านภาพรวมภาษาไทย](README.th.md)

> **Status notice:** These are research directions, not claims of completed experiments, validated field performance, commercial readiness or confirmed novelty.

## Six research projects

### 01 — Coconut Harvest AI
**Agricultural computer vision and coconut maturity assessment.** Studies image-based classification of defined maturity classes, dataset quality, annotation consistency, evaluation and lightweight CPU inference.

### 02 — PUI89 Sentinel
**Multimodal perception, evidence-aware AI and decision support.** Studies permitted information modalities, uncertainty communication, evidence provenance and human review. Each study must define its application scope and verify data rights before use.

**Scope exclusion:** Narcotics detection and screening, related datasets, X-ray screening and associated forensic spectral research are outside the active scope of this programme.

### 03 — AI HOME
**Indoor assistive robotics and reliable embodied AI.** Studies indoor perception, environmental understanding, human-aware assistance, safe action proposals and dependable operation for a compact mobile assistant.

### 04 — AI NOMAD
**Outdoor mobile robotics and robust perception.** Studies outdoor context, perception under changing conditions, uncertainty handling and safe behaviour for a compact mobile assistant distinct from AI HOME.

### 05 — AI Robot Car
**Smart mobility, vehicle perception and safety-aware intelligence.** Studies road-scene understanding, obstacle and hazard awareness, environmental context, warnings and reliable edge inference. This research does not imply autonomous steering, throttle or braking.

### 06 — EasyRide AI Step-Through Scooter
**AI-assisted two-wheel mobility and road-safety intelligence.** Studies rider-support information, road and obstacle awareness, environmental warnings, vehicle condition monitoring and edge AI. This research does not imply autonomous throttle, steering or braking.

## Shared research method

Each project is expected to document:
1. Literature review and the state of the art.
2. A specific, testable research gap; novelty is not assumed.
3. Data provenance, ownership, licence, permitted use and limitations.
4. Research questions, methodology, baselines, metrics, data splits and hardware.
5. Evaluation results, uncertainty, failure cases and limitations.
6. Reproducibility materials where rights permit.
7. A clear distinction between concepts, hypotheses, planned experiments, measured results and independently verified evidence.

## Publication and responsible-use policy

- No unsupported performance, safety, field-validation or commercial-readiness claims.
- No guarantee of novelty or journal quartile; publication depends on completed work and peer review.
- Third-party datasets, images, models, code and publications require licence and rights checks.
- Do not publish personal, confidential, restricted or unauthorized information.
- Access to a dataset or model does not automatically grant redistribution rights.
- Record limitations and provenance for every study.

## Project status

Inclusion here means a project belongs to the research portfolio. It does not mean experiments are complete or a manuscript is ready for submission. Detailed project records will be added after scope, methodology, rights and evidence have been reviewed.
